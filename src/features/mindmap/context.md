# mindmap

UI-only feature: "Inspect Roots", the deck's knowledge as a mindmap growing out of its tree.

## Owned tables
None. Never calls actions; pages pass the tree and item levels as props.

## Tree & roots chart (what the deck page shows)
The deck page's "Tree & roots" section is `RootChart`: the whole root hierarchy on a zoomable, pannable canvas. It replaced the older canvas `RootMap` there; `RootMap`, its layout (`layoutMindmap`), cards (`NodePill`, `StatementCard`) and scene helpers are still in the feature and exported, but no page mounts them.

| Export | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| `RootChart({ nodes, levels, treeType, title?, surface?, emptyLabel?, onManage?, onAddRoot?, ownerTools?, practice?, statements? })` | Client. A diorama: the tree **stands free** at the top (the page's drawing in `surface.content`, no box; it sways with `.mg-tree-sway` about its trunk's foot, `surface.base`, default 50% / 90%) on a soil ring on the ground line, with a floating label capsule beside the trunk (`title`, `stageLabel`, overall mastery). A tapering **taproot** drops from the foot of the trunk and branches into curving tendrils to the top-level roots; **every tier of roots** hangs beneath (sub-roots under their parent) as compact cards, each tendril tinted by the child's mastery and thinner with each tier. The frame (`.mg-chart-ground` in globals.css) paints sky above the ground line and soil below, following the camera through `--mg-ground`. **Statements are never drawn on the canvas**: a card shows the root's title, a progress ring, a status badge (`rootStatus`), its own statement count and its sub-root count; the card or its "View" button opens `NodeInspector` (the side drawer) with that root's statements. Top-level roots also carry 💧 Drill / ⚔️ Compete when `practice` is passed (a sub-root is drilled from its drawer); owners get the hover ✏️ / 🗑️ / ⚙️ tools on every card. Corner toolbar: zoom out, zoom %, zoom in, fit to screen. Never calls actions |
| `layoutRootTree(nodes)`, `CHART`, `tendrilPath`, `type RootTreeLayout` | Pure tidy-tree geometry (`hooks/orgChartLayout.ts`, tested in `__tests__/orgChart.test.ts`; also `taprootPath`): the tree's drawing box (`tree`, inside the chart's bounds so fitting never clips the crown), the trunk's foot on the ground line (`base`), the `taproot`, the floating `label`, one card per root at `depth` 0, 1, 2…, each root centred over its sub-roots, subtrees never sharing x-ranges (no overlaps for any shape), and one cubic curve per card from its parent's foot to its head |
| `rootStatus(mastery)`, `ROOT_STATUS_LABEL`, `type RootStatus`, `subRootCount(node)` | The badge: `empty` (no statements), `seed` (0), `growing`, `thriving` (from two thirds of the way), `mighty` (5/5); and how many roots sit under a root |
| `MIN_ZOOM` (0.5), `MAX_ZOOM` (1.8), `clampZoom`, `zoomAround`, `panBy`, `clampPan`, `fitCamera`, `cameraTransform`, `type Camera` | Pure camera maths (`hooks/chartCamera.ts`, tested): the chart is one element drawn with `translate3d(x, y, 0) scale(k)`; zooming keeps the point under the cursor still; panning is free in both directions but always leaves part of the chart in the frame; fit shows the whole chart (never above 100%) |
| — `hooks/useChartCamera.ts` | The gestures: drag the background to pan (a press becomes a drag after 6 px, and the click that ends a drag is swallowed, so cards and buttons still click), wheel / trackpad pinch to zoom at the cursor (at the zoom limit the wheel is left to scroll the page), two-finger pinch, and the toolbar buttons. The camera lives in a ref and is written to the chart's `style.transform` once per animation frame, so panning and zooming never re-render React; only the zoom badge is state. Not the shared `useCamera` (that one scrolls a container, starts at 35% and needs Ctrl for the wheel) |

## Rules
- Cards show the statement text (`content_nodes.content`) to everyone who can read it: the owner wrote it (or cloned it) and already sees it in the Tree Workshop; visitors read shared trees. "Statement n" is only the fallback for a missing or blank text (`statementLabel`). Answers still stay secret where it matters: drill questions never carry `correctTag`, and grading happens on the server
- Per-statement drilling doesn't exist: rounds start from a top-level root pill (or the inspector), never from a statement card
- Mighty Root = the node's own statements all at 5/5 (same rule as the profile stats)

## Scene
`hooks/scene.ts`: `sceneGeometry(layout, surface, sideMargin?)` gives a fixed-size world (soil 480px past the roots on each side), the ground line, the tree box on the trunk, and a `focus` box for the camera to fit. `rootStroke`, `conduitPath`, `groundPath` are pure helpers.
