# garden

The tree surface: the Farm World (a 16 × 16 isometric grid with a 🏪 Shop, Hay Day style), the classic
grid of decks and the single tree. Trees and progress come from the page as props; the farm's own
placements are this feature's table.

## Owned tables
`garden_placements` (migration `20260930000000_farm_grid.sql`; DATABASE.md "Farm Grid Feature"). Written only through
`purchase_and_place_item()` (`placeFarmItem`), `move_garden_placement()` (`moveFarmPlacement`, migration 14) and owner deletes (`removeFarmPlacement`).

## Vivid casual-game look (supersedes the colour notes above)
- `lowPolyKit` lighting: `lit()` saturates each tone first (`vivid()` in `shared/lib/color.ts`), then mixes sunlit planes towards a warm `SUNLIGHT` and shaded ones towards a deep indigo `DEEP_SHADOW` (rich, coloured shadows, never grey). `Bevel` draws crisp warm-white highlight lines; `IsoBox` tops, `Cylinder` cap rims, `Cone` sunny edges and `GableRoof` ridges / eaves get them automatically. `FootprintShadow({ w, h })` is a two-diamond ambient-occlusion shadow under a footprint (every 2 × 2 building has one)
- `PowerBuildings.tsx` exports `PALETTE` (saturated named colours) and `smoke()` (voluminous puffs). Small Windmill: ivory limestone, honey-wood balcony with flower planters and glowing lanterns, terracotta cone, golden-oak sails with white fabric panels. Grand Windmill: cobblestone base, timber decking, glowing amber windows, brass cogs, crimson dome, double-braced sails with red-and-white striped canvas. Furniture Factory: cedar siding, cobalt tin roof, amber lumber, safety-striped saw bench, silver saw throwing sawdust sparks (`.mg-spark`) while powered. Candy Factory: mint walls, hot-pink roof, glowing gumdrops and a citrus lollipop on the ridge, gloss candy-cane chimney with white smoke, magenta candies on the conveyor. Seaport: navy pilings over azure ripples (`.mg-wave`), diagonal black-and-yellow curbs, hazard-yellow crane, vermilion / royal blue / emerald containers, freighter with a white bridge, red hull stripe and a foaming wake (`.mg-wake`)

- **PNG sprites** (supersede the vector drawings of these three in the farm and the Shop): the Small Windmill, Candy Factory and Furniture Factory draw as static images, `components/BuildingImages.tsx` `BuildingImage({ kind, powered })` via `ItemDrawing`. `BUILDING_IMAGES` gives each sprite's file, pixel size, ground anchor (the base centre, placed on the footprint's ground point (0, 0), so depth sorting is unchanged) and scale (0.3: each base fits the 2 × 2 diamond).
